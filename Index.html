Key takeaways

  1. Crop is tracking 16% under budget in 2026 (6,569 mt vs 7,831 mt to May). FY2025 closed at 15.37 mt/ha, below the PA's 16.00 forecast.
  2. Cost per tonne still beats budget (RM266 vs RM286), mainly because manuring, pruning and pest spend are delayed, not because the estate is efficient.
  3. Manuring is the recurring weak spot: 72% in 2024, 80% in 2025, 54% of H1 2026. Agronomist calls it a disappointment.
  4. Harvesters are 6 short of requirement (49 vs 55). 6 workers absconded in 5 months.
  5. 62% of the estate is over 20 years old and Ganoderma-hit. Yield is structurally capped until P98C replanting.
